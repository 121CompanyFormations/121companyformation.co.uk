Platinum Package

The Platinum Package offers a comprehensive set of services that takes care of the many statutory requirements for newly formed companies including filing your first year's confirmation statement as well as registering for the company for VAT . More than providing you a hassle-free set of services, this package leaves you free to focus on making your business a success knowing that everything that needs to be done to set up your company has been handled by a team of company formation experts.

WHAT DOES THE PLATINUM PACKAGE INCLUDE?

A highly personalised service that will not only take care of all the necessary documentation at the time of incorporation but also ensure that the company's first year confirmation statement is filed and that the company is registered with HMRC for VAT purposes. The Platinum Package includes:
  • An incorporated limited company that is ready to begin trading
  • Officers of the company appointed
  • Ordinary shares issued
  • A PDF file containing the Certificate of Incorporation, the Memorandum and Articles of Association and the share certificate/s
  • Registration for VAT with HMRC
  • Free filing of the company’s Confirmation Statement for the first year
  • Free access to your company management portal
  • Free fast-track referral service for business banking facilities
