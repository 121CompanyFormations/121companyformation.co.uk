Business Telephone Services

We offer business call answering services which ensure that every call to your company is dealt with professionally and promptly.

WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF A BUSINESS TELEPHONE SERVICE?

  • Never miss a call from a customer
  • Provide a better customer experience, with every call, answered, not routed to voicemail
  • Reduce overhead costs by eliminating the need to employ a receptionist
  • Ability to focus on what matters most - running your business!
  • Leverage a London or regional landline telephone number for your company

WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE TELEPHONE ANSWERING SERVICE FOR SMALL BUSINESS?

The service includes:
  • A business telephone number unique to your company
  • An ‘A Plus’ answering service
  • A call forwarding service
  • Messages forwarded to you via email for your prompt attention
