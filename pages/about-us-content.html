About us

Helping entrepreneurs form and grow UK limited companies — with clear packages and support after incorporation.

Who we are

UK company formation specialists

121 Company Formation is amongst the UK’s leading company formation service providers, offering a fast, cost-effective online limited company registration service, as well as a full range of bookkeeping and accounting services to customers who wish to register a business and operate a limited company in the UK.

Simply complete the online application via our easy-to-use online portal. We will thoroughly review your application before submitting it to Companies House on your behalf. Typically, your limited company registration will be complete — and your company ready to trade — typically within 24 business hours.

Our mission

Start fast. Run with less hassle.

Our goal is to help entrepreneurs start their UK limited companies and to provide a wide array of business support services so they can operate as efficiently, and as hassle-free as possible, while focusing on what matters most — getting their companies up and running and on the road to success.
