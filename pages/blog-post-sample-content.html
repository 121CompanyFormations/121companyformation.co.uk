Introduction to company formation

Company formation involves incorporating a business as a limited company and registering it as a legal entity with Companies House. Once formed, the company can trade, own assets, and enter contracts in its own name.

Benefits of incorporating

  • Limited liability for shareholders
  • A clearer, more professional structure for clients and banks
  • Potential tax planning options compared with sole trading
  • Easier to bring in investors or transfer ownership later

Could my application be rejected?

Applications can be delayed or rejected if required information is missing, inconsistent, or if the proposed company name is unavailable or uses sensitive words without approval. Professional checks before filing help reduce those risks.

Keep the strongest differentiators — professional checks before filing — visible next to the offer.
