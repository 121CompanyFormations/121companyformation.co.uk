Company structures

Which structure should I choose?

A short guide to the four formation paths available on this page.

Limited by Shares

The standard UK private limited company. Owned by shareholders and run by directors, with limited liability. Best for most trading businesses.

Limited Liability Partnership

A flexible partnership structure with limited liability for members. Often used by professional firms and joint ventures.

Limited by Guarantee

Typically used by non-profits, clubs and membership organisations. There are no shares — members guarantee a nominal amount.

International

Packages designed for customers forming a UK company from overseas, including options with registered office and compliance support.

FAQ

Package questions

Answers about pricing, inclusions and what happens after you order.

What is included in every package?

All packages include complete company formation, Certificate of Incorporation, share certificate (where applicable), Memorandum and Articles, and electronic filing with Companies House. Optional extras such as address services, confirmation statements and accountancy are listed separately in each package column.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Package prices are shown upfront as one-off fees. The Companies House registration fee is included in our formation pricing, so you will not find a separate Companies House charge added at checkout for standard registration.

How long does incorporation take?

Applications are filed electronically with Companies House. Typical incorporation is within 24 business hours, subject to Companies House workload. Applications can only be submitted after KYC (proof of ID and address) is complete.

What is the difference between Bronze, Gold, Platinum and Diamond?

Bronze covers core formation. Gold and Platinum add more post-incorporation support such as confirmation statement filing and VAT registration. Diamond is the fullest package, including accountancy-related extras. Use the comparison table above to see exactly which inclusions apply to each tier.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can purchase additional services after incorporation — for example registered office address, confirmation statement filing, VAT registration or accountancy — from our services pages or by contacting the team.

What if Companies House rejects my application?

We will email you automatically. You can log back in, correct the details and resubmit. Companies House usually keeps the document ID open for three days after a rejection, so changes should be made promptly.

Do I need a UK registered office address?

Yes. Every UK company must have a valid UK registered office. If you do not have one — or you are an overseas customer — we can provide a Central London registered office address as an optional service.
