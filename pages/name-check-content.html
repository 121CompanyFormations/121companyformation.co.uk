Name check FAQ

Before you continue

Is the company name check free?

Yes. You can search as many variations as you like, with no account or payment required.

If it says available, is the name guaranteed?

No name can be reserved in the UK. Companies House makes the final decision when your application is reviewed. We check again before filing and will contact you if there is a problem.

Do I need to add Limited or Ltd?

Private limited companies must end in Limited or Ltd. You can type the trading name — we ensure the correct ending is used when we submit to Companies House.

What happens after I click Continue?

Choose a formation package, then complete your online order with director, shareholder and address details. We file electronically and most companies are incorporated within about 24 hours*.

*Subject to Companies House workload on working days.
