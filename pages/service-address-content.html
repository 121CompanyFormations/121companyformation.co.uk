Service Address

At the time of incorporation, all officers of a company must provide both their home address and a service address. These addresses can be one and the same.

Legally, unlike the company’s registered office address, officers’ home addresses and service addresses do not need to be based in the UK.

WHY USE A SERVICE ADDRESS:

  • The service address serves as an alternative to company officers using their personal residential address for the receipt of official or statutory company documents
  • Protect the privacy of company officers’ residential addresses and instead have company correspondence, and unsolicited mail sent to the service address
  • Have the company’s official correspondence forwarded to an address of your choice, or sent to you via email
