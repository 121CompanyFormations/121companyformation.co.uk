FAQ

Common questions

Answers from our knowledge base. If you need something else, email us and we will help.

How long does it take to form a company?

Our system uses the Electronic Filing facilities at Companies House. When you place an order, your request is sent electronically. Typical incorporation is within 24 business hours, subject to Companies House workload.

Do I need a solicitor or accountant to form a company?

No — we can handle the whole process from start to finish. We also provide company formation services to many accountants and solicitors.

What details do I need to form a limited company?

You will need a registered office address, details of directors and shareholders, share capital information, and a chosen company name that Companies House will accept.

Can I choose any name I want for my company?

You cannot register the same name as another company, and certain sensitive words need approval. Use our name check to review availability before you order.
