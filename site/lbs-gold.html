Gold Package

The Gold Package is the best option for those who are looking for a superior experience when setting up their company with the full support of a highly experienced company formation facility.

Being a more prestige package, the Gold Package offers you all the benefits of the Bronze and Silver Packages plus free filing of the company’s Confirmation Statement for the first year.

WHAT DOES THE GOLD PACKAGE INCLUDE?

A highly personalized service that will not only take care of all the necessary documentation at the time of incorporation but will also provide full support throughout the entire process and beyond. The Gold Package includes:

  • An incorporated limited company that is ready to begin trading
  • Officers of the company appointed
  • Ordinary shares issued
  • A PDF file containing the Certificate of Incorporation, the Memorandum and Articles of Association, and the share certificate/s
  • Free filing of the company’s Confirmation Statement for the first year
  • Free access to your company management portal
  • Free fast-track referral service for business banking facilities

UK and international companies and individuals looking to set up a for-profit company in the UK.

Buy Now

We offer a very quick service – your company will be usually formed within three to four hours of placing your order, subject to Companies House response times.

THE PROCESS

  1. Use our free tool to do a company name search to check that your chosen company name is available. If it is not, we will recommend a few alternatives
  2. Complete the self-guided company incorporation application form with details of the company secretary, directors, shareholders, registered address and director service address , if applicable (Please see FAQs below for detailed explanations of these)
  3. We’ll submit the application to Companies House on your behalf, along with the appropriate fee
  4. We will contact you via email (typically within 24 business hours), once your company has been incorporated and registered at Companies House.
