KNOWLEDGE BASE FAQs

To register a company you will need:
  • An available company name
  • The company’s business activity
  • A registered office address in the UK (Don’t yet have one? We can set up a UK registered office address for you!)
  • The names, dates of birth, contact addresses and other details of the company directors, shareholders, People with Significant Control (PSC) and the beneficial owners of the company, i.e. those owning more than 25% of the issued shares
  • All company formation UK and accountancy clients to complete KYC process (Proof of ID and address) in advance of service, which may lengthen the incorporation process beyond 24 hours.
It is not a legal requirement to have a business bank account for your registered UK company. However, operating your company without a business bank account is likely to pose several major challenges!

Our partnerships with some of the UK’s major financial institutions provide you with access to a range of financial products and services to help you get your business off to a great start. That includes a fast-track business bank account service with every company formation.

A limited company by shares can be set up by just one person.

Limited companies have more filing and accounting responsibilities than sole trader businesses. These include:
  •   Annual Confirmation Statement
  •   Company Tax Returns
  •   Annual 'statutory' year end accounts
  •   Vat returns, if VAT registered.
With our hassle-free, no-fuss application process, after you have completed our KYC process (proof of ID and address) it takes just 15 minutes to complete and submit your limited company registration application. Once your application is submitted to Companies House, they typically takes 24 business hours to complete the process and register your limited company or LLP subject to their workload
The moment Companies House incorporates your company, we will let you know! We will inform you via the email address you provided on your application. The confirmation email will contain your Companies House Authentication Code and all your official company documents including:
  •   Your memorandum
  •   Articles of association
  •   Share certificate(s)
  •   And most importantly, your certificate of incorporation.
  • looking to set up a for-profit company: A Limited by share company is the most popular choice for this purpose. Limited by share companies are owned by shareholder(s)/subscriber(s) and are run by director(s)
  • Non-profit organizations: a Limited by guarantee company is most suitable for charitable organizations
  • Professionals who require limited liability status: Professionals who operate as a partnership and who require limited liability for its members (i.e. the LLP is a separate legal entity and, while the LLP itself will be liable for the full extent of its assets, the liability of the members will be limited)
